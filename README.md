# Sarfi Support · دعم صرفي

Official public support and privacy pages for **Sarfi**, a private, local-first spending tracker for iPhone, widgets and Apple Watch.

- [Support website](https://semooo.github.io/Sarfi-Support/)
- [Privacy policy](https://semooo.github.io/Sarfi-Support/#privacy)
- [Report a bug](https://github.com/SeMoOo/Sarfi-Support/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)
- [Request a feature](https://github.com/SeMoOo/Sarfi-Support/issues/new?template=feature_request.md)

Issues are public. Do not post transaction details, bank messages, card or account numbers, OTPs, exports, phone numbers or other sensitive information.
